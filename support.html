Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn coins?

Tap the coin to flip it. If it lands on heads, you earn coins based on your current upgrade level.

What are upgrades?

Upgrades improve your coin — increasing your odds of landing heads, the payout per flip, your multiplier, and your toss speed.

How do streaks work?

Consecutive heads builds a winning streak. Hit milestone streaks (15, 20, 25) for massive bonus payouts. If your streak breaks, you can watch an ad to continue it.

Why am I seeing ads?

Biased Coin Flip is free to play and supported by ads. Rewarded ads (to continue streaks) are always optional.

The app is not working properly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure you have the latest version installed from the App Store. If it still doesn't work, contact us via email.
