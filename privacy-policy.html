Privacy Policy for Biased Coin Flip

Last updated: February 2026

Introduction

Biased Coin Flip ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application ("App") available on iOS and Android platforms.

Information We Collect

Biased Coin Flip is a casual coin-flipping game that does not require user accounts or collect personal information directly. However, we use third-party services that may collect information to provide and improve our services.

Google AdMob

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and use information about your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address
  • Device type, model, and operating system
  • App usage data and interactions with advertisements
  • General location data (not precise location)

This information is used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance. For more information about how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, we request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You may choose to allow or deny this request. If you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to your interests.

Consent Management (GDPR/CCPA)

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent for users in regions where data privacy regulations apply, including the European Economic Area (GDPR) and California (CCPA). When required, a consent dialog will be presented allowing you to choose your preferences for data collection and personalized advertising.

Local Storage

Our app uses on-device storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android) to save your game progress, including your coin balance, upgrade levels, and game statistics. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Save your game progress locally on your device
  • Improve our app and user experience
  • Analyze app performance and fix issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Google AdMob — For advertising services. AdMob's use of information is governed by their Privacy Policy and Data Processing Terms.
  • SKAdNetwork Partners — Apple's SKAdNetwork is used for privacy-preserving ad attribution on iOS. This does not share personally identifiable information.
  • Legal Requirements — When required by law or to protect our rights.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Control tracking (iOS) — When prompted by the App Tracking Transparency dialog, choose "Ask App Not to Track" to prevent IDFA access. You can also change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Manage ad preferences (Android) — Go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Withdraw consent — If you provided consent via the GDPR/CCPA dialog, you can manage your preferences within the app or by reinstalling it.
  • Request information — You can request information about data we collect by contacting us.
  • Request deletion — You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.
  • Uninstall the app — Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored game data from your device.

Data Retention

Game data stored on your device persists until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's data retention policies.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All game data is stored locally on your device. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information.

International Users

If you are using our app from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries where Google operates its advertising infrastructure. By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Third-Party Links

Our app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.
